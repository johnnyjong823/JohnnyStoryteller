# 不敢睡覺的小貓頭鷹 — 繪圖提示詞

> 產圖流程見 [docs/04-圖片製作指南](../../../../docs/04-圖片製作指南.md)。
> **先產角色設定表那一張，確認角色長相，再往下做。**
> 12 張圖並排看，如果有一張角色明顯不一樣，重產那一張，不要將就。

## 風格字串

每一張圖都要原封不動附加這一段，整篇故事都不要改：

```
soft children's picture book illustration, gouache texture, warm muted color palette, gentle rounded shapes, soft diffused lighting, no text, no letters, no watermark, 4:3 aspect ratio
```

## 角色設定表

**嘟嘟** — 先產這一張，之後每一張都掛它當參考圖。

```
character reference sheet, a small round baby owl named Dudu, fluffy pale cream feathers with soft caramel-brown speckles on the wings, oversized round amber eyes, a tiny grey beak, two small tufted ear feathers that curl slightly, short stubby wings, standing on two small orange feet,
three views: front view, side view, three-quarter view,
plain light grey background, full body, neutral pose, no shadows,
soft children's picture book illustration, gouache texture, warm muted color palette, gentle rounded shapes, soft diffused lighting, no text, no letters, no watermark, 4:3 aspect ratio
```
